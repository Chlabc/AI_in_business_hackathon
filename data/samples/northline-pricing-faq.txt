Northline — Internal commission & listing FAQ
=============================================
Agency name: Northline
Vertical: Australian residential real estate

COMMISSION (ex GST; marketing separate)
---------------------------------------
Standard commission: 2.5%
Floor (principal approval required below): 2%
Competitor often quotes: 1.75%

Notes for agents:
- Do not open with a discount. Discover what the lower quote includes first.
- Clarify appraisal quality, marketing spend, and negotiation support before moving fee.
- Never invent a commission below the 2% floor without principal approval.

VALUE ANCHORS
-------------
- Local comparable sales to support the appraisal
- A tailored property marketing and inspection plan
- Buyer qualification, negotiation support and regular seller updates
- Clear campaign timeline with open-home cadence
- Transparent marketing schedule and spend options

FEE OBJECTION — APPROVED PLAY (optional AI cue patch)
-----------------------------------------------------
When a seller says commission is too high vs another agency:
1) Ask what the lower quote includes (marketing, appraisal depth, after-sale support).
2) Anchor on local evidence, the campaign plan, and negotiation.
3) Review campaign scope before discussing a concession.
Never: match the competitor % in the first reply.

LISTING / FAQ DUMP
------------------
Q: Is an appraisal a formal valuation?
A: No — it is an estimate based on local comparables and property condition, not a bank valuation.

Q: Who pays for marketing?
A: Marketing is separate from commission unless a campaign package says otherwise.

Q: Can we do a no-obligation appraisal?
A: Yes — with agreed success criteria; do not pressure sellers who clearly refuse.
