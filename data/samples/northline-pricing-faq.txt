Northline — Internal pricing & value FAQ
========================================
Company name: Northline
Vertical: B2B workflow SaaS for mid-market ops teams

PRICING
-------
List seat price: $135/seat/mo
Floor (approval required below): $95
Competitor often quotes: $85/seat/mo

Notes for AEs:
- Do not open with a discount. Discover what “too expensive” means first.
- Annual prepay and multi-year can trade before touching list.
- Never invent pricing below the $95 floor without VP Finance.

VALUE ANCHORS
-------------
- Time-to-value under 14 days with a dedicated CSM
- SOC2 Type II + SSO included on every plan
- 99.9% uptime SLA with service credits
- Migration playbook for teams leaving spreadsheets or legacy tools
- Shared success plan with quarterly business reviews

FEE OBJECTION — APPROVED PLAY (optional AI cue patch)
-----------------------------------------------------
When a buyer says list is too high vs a competitor quote:
1) Clarify what failed rollout costs them this quarter.
2) Anchor on CSM + time-to-value, not features.
3) Offer annual prepay before moving seat price.
Never: match the competitor number in the first reply.

SECURITY / FAQ DUMP
-------------------
Q: Do you support SSO?
A: Yes — SAML and OIDC on Growth and above.

Q: Where is data hosted?
A: US-East primary; EU residency available on Enterprise.

Q: Can we pilot?
A: 30-day pilot on one team with success criteria agreed up front — not unlimited free seats.
