Northline Realty — Firm knowledge pack (sample upload)
======================================================
Agency name: Northline
Vertical: Australian residential real estate
Document type: Internal playbook / commission & listing FAQ
Audience: Listing agents and principals
Effective: Sample for Cornerman Playbook import testing

1. COMMISSION CARD (ex GST; marketing billed separately)
-------------------------------------------------------
Standard commission: 2.8%
Floor (principal approval required below): 2.2%
Competitor often quotes: 1.9%

Notes for agents:
- Do not open with a discount. Discover what the lower quote includes first.
- Clarify appraisal quality, marketing spend, and negotiation support before moving fee.
- Never invent a commission below the 2.2% floor without principal approval.
- Annual / multi-property packages may trade scope before any % movement.

2. VALUE ANCHORS (use these in appraisals and fee talks)
-------------------------------------------------------
- Fresh suburb comps refreshed weekly for appraisal conversations
- Open-home and digital campaign pack included in listing kickoff
- Negotiation support through unconditional exchange
- Weekly written seller updates during the campaign
- Clear campaign timeline with inspection cadence and vendor reporting
- Transparent marketing schedule and spend options (print + digital)

3. FEE OBJECTION — APPROVED PLAY
-------------------------------
When a seller says commission is too high vs another agency:
1) Ask what the lower quote includes (marketing, appraisal depth, after-sale support).
2) Anchor on local evidence, the campaign plan, and negotiation support.
3) Review campaign scope before discussing any concession.
Never: match the competitor 1.9% in the first reply.
Example line: "Before we talk percentage — what does their 1.9% include for marketing and negotiation through to exchange?"

4. OTHER COMMON OBJECTIONS (short cues)
--------------------------------------
Already have an agent:
- Respect the relationship; ask when their agreement ends; offer a no-obligation appraisal later.

Not interested / not selling now:
- Stop pitching; ask about timeframe; seek permission for a later market check-in.

Need to think it over:
- Summarise value; agree a clear next step and follow-up date.

5. LISTING / FAQ
----------------
Q: Is an appraisal a formal valuation?
A: No — it is an estimate based on local comparables and property condition, not a bank valuation.

Q: Who pays for marketing?
A: Marketing is separate from commission unless a campaign package says otherwise.

Q: Can we do a no-obligation appraisal?
A: Yes — with agreed success criteria; do not pressure sellers who clearly refuse.

Q: Can we go below 2.2%?
A: Only with principal approval. Do not invent a lower rate in the room.

Q: How do we talk about GST?
A: Quote commission ex GST unless the seller asks for inclusive figures; be explicit which you mean.
